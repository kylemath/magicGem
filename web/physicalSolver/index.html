Magic squares as spectral height fields

A normal magic square is a zero-mean permutation over a lattice in the (n²-2n-1)-dimensional nullspace of the rationalized magic-line Laplacian L̃_n = P_{1^⊥} A_n^T A_n P_{1^⊥}. Because its 3D spatial covariance is universal across all semi-magic squares, Paper D studies the exact integer spectrum of L̃_n, multiscale window entropies H̃_p(k), the Concentric Shell Quadrupole Identity, and cooperative (2,2) relaxation.
