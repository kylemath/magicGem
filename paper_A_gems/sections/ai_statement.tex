\section*{Use of AI systems}
\label{sec:ai_statement}

The author conceived the Magic Gem construction in a spoken conversation with a chatbot (OpenAI's ChatGPT), used as a sounding board; the transcript is available from the author.
All versions of this paper were prepared with substantial help from generative AI, which earlier versions did not state.
Versions 1--4 were prepared with a mix of OpenAI's ChatGPT, Anthropic's Claude (Sonnet and Opus, used in the Cursor editor) and Google's Gemini, used for discussion, code and tests over the three years of the project.
This version was restructured and largely rewritten with Anthropic's Claude, used through the agentic tool Claude Code in October 2026, which wrote and ran the code in \texttt{anc/}, proposed and wrote out proofs, and drafted the text; separate AI sessions acted as adversarial referees.
Several errors in earlier versions were found in that process and are corrected here.
The author directed the work and is responsible for the content; AI systems are not authors.
Every number in the paper is recomputed by the scripts in \texttt{anc/}, the order-$4$ results by exact enumeration of all $880$ squares.
The author has checked every proof in Sections~2--6 and the appendices by hand.

\paragraph{Changes from earlier versions.}
Versions 1--4 contained the following errors and overstatements, which are corrected or withdrawn here.
(i)~The ``Complete Energy Characterization Theorem'' presented in version~4 as the main theoretical contribution is immediate from the definitions: the energy is a sum of squared line-sum deviations, so it vanishes exactly on magic squares. It is not presented as a result here.
(ii)~Table~2 of version~4 gave about $3.4\times10^7$ essentially different magic squares of order five; the correct number is $275{,}305{,}224$ \cite{gardner1976}, and $3.4\times10^7$ is that number divided by $8$.
(iii)~The same table gave $8$, $12$ and $16$ as the ``typical'' numbers of hull vertices for orders $3$, $4$ and $5$. For order four the number ranges from $8$ to $16$ (\Cref{tab:census} of this version), and the Siamese gem of order five has $12$ vertices (\Cref{thm:siamese}).
(iv)~The energy-landscape statistics of version~4 (its Section~4.5, including the ``landscape entropy'' and the claim that it is scale-invariant) are not carried over.
(v)~The statistics in Table~3 of version~4 weighted the diagonal terms of the energy differently from its stated definition. That table also reported that $6\times10^7$ uniformly random arrangements of order four contained no zero of the four-term energy other than magic squares; the zeros are the arrangements whose first moments vanish and whose two diagonals sum to $34$. An exact count gives $14{,}639{,}440$ such zeros among the $16!$ arrangements, $7{,}040$ of them magic (\texttt{anc/energy\_zeros\_count.py}), so about $42$ non-magic zeros were to be expected, and a rerun with the same number of samples finds $45$ (\texttt{anc/v4\_sampling\_recheck.py}). That statement of version~4 is withdrawn.
(vi)~The hardware stated in version~4 was incorrect; the computations were run on an Apple M4 Max.
