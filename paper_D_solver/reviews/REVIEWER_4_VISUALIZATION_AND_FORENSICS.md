## 1. General Assessment & Visual Storyboard Progression Audit (Figures 1–9)

This manuscript is a tour de force of interactive mathematical physics, combinatorial landscape analysis, and algorithmic engineering. Starting from a deceptively simple pedagogical question—*can physical forces pull a scrambled grid of numbers into a magic square?*—the paper constructs an unbroken causal arc across five regimes:
1. **Linear-algebraic insufficiency of rigid-body balance** (Section 2, Figure 1, Table 2): why zero 3D gravitational tilt and exact main diagonals leave $2n-4$ polynomial row/column modes unconstrained ($S_{\mathrm{low}}$ at $\mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{line}} = 1{,}584$), and why restricting $L_n = A_n^\top A_n$ to the zero-mean hyperplane $\mathbf{1}^\perp$ collapses irrational eigenvalues into exact integers with universal mean $\bar{\mu}(\widetilde{L}_n) = 2$.
2. **Internal texture and multipole geometry of the ground-state manifold** (Section 3, Figures 2–3, Tables 3–4): how sliding $k\times k$ window entropies $\widetilde{H}_p(k)$, interior-to-toroidal $2\times 2$ quartet equivalence (Lemma 3.1), and the Concentric Shell Quadrupole Identity $M_{xyz}(S) = -8\,Q_{\mathrm{corner}}(S) - 3\,Q_{\mathrm{edge}}(S)$ (Proposition 3.2) classify all $880$ Frénicle order-$4$ magic squares and explain the $56 = 48 + 8$ octupole zeros.
3. **Combinatorial trapping and Kempe-like $(2,2)$ plaquette unlocking** (Section 4, Figures 4–5, Table 5): why the quadratic self-energy recoil $c_{ab}d^2$ freezes single swaps in the $8$ antipodal traps $D_4 \cdot S_{\mathrm{trap}}^{(3)}$ across $25.47\%$ of $S_9$ and $81.75\%$ of $S_{16}$, and why a unique row-preserving $(2,2)$ double swap $(6\leftrightarrow 7)(3\leftrightarrow 4)$ unlocks $S_9$ to $100\%$.
4. **Continuous amplitude-blending pathology vs. multi-exchange dimensional relief** (Section 5, Figures 6–7, Tables 6–8): why continuous 2D Gaussian bubbles in `bubble-trinity.html` lower $\mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{ind}}(t)$ by $37.5\%$ yet recover $0.0\%$ magic squares by parking on cell borders, whereas discrete Multi-Exchange Iterated Local Search (ME-ILS) achieves $100\%$ synthesis and exposes a frustration peak at $n=5$ ($8.03$ restarts) followed by nullspace relief as $f_{\mathrm{null}}(n) = 1 - 2/(n-1)^2 \to 1$.
5. **Asymptotic plateau scaling to $n=50$ and rank-$(n+2)$ BLAS bilinear acceleration to $n=100$** (Section 6, Figures 8–9, Tables 9–10): demonstrating across all $48$ consecutive orders $n=3\dots 50$ that basin-hopping restarts remain bounded on an order-invariant plateau (median $4.32$ restarts) while per-restart descents grow linearly as $3.52\,n + 20.86$, and proving the Bilinear Plaquette Identity (Theorem 6.1) to achieve empirical $O(n^{2.93})$ per-restart scaling and a $34.7\times\text{--}50.2\times$ speedup over all-pairs search at $n=46, 48, 100$.
