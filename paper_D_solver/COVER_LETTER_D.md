Dear Editors,

Please find enclosed our manuscript, *"Spectral Structure, Multiscale Window Entropies, and Multi-Exchange Energy Landscapes of Magic Squares,"* which we submit for consideration for publication. This work forms the fourth installment of our *Magic Gems* series (following Papers I–III on polyhedral convex hulls, bivariate polynomial moments, and 3D rotational/structural mechanics), shifting the focus from the static geometry of known magic squares to the combinatorial energy landscape $\mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{line}}(S) = \mathbf{z}^\top L_n \mathbf{z}$ on the symmetric group $S_{n^2}$. Whereas classical constructive methods (Siamese, doubly-even mask, Conway LUX) generate magic squares in $O(n^2)$ operations and constraint-based local search (CBLS) heuristics target defective lines directly, our goal here is to characterize the global and local topological structure of $\mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{line}}$ across scales—determining why continuous 2D spatial kernel interpolation and $2$- or $3$-exchange local searches get trapped, and how $4$-element $(2,2)$ plaquette moves resolve those traps.

We first review the Rook's-graph-plus-diagonals decomposition of the line-incidence operator $L_n = A_n^\top A_n$ and show (Proposition 2.1) that restricting $L_n$ to the zero-mean permutation hyperplane $\mathbf{1}^\perp$ via $\widetilde{L}_n = P_{\mathbf{1}^\perp} L_n P_{\mathbf{1}^\perp}$ decouples the constant mode and confines the rank-$2$ diagonal perturbation inside the Rook's-graph kernel, reducing the irrational eigenvalues $\lambda_\pm(n)$ to exact integers: $\{0^{[n^2-2n-1]}, (n-2)^{[1]}, n^{[2n-1]}\}$ for even $n \ge 4$ and $\{0^{[n^2-2n-1]}, (n-1)^{[2]}, n^{[2n-2]}\}$ for odd $n \ge 3$. Both the trace identity $\operatorname{tr}(\widetilde{L}_n) = 2(n^2-1)$ and finite-population sampling without replacement yield a universal mean eigenvalue $\bar{\mu}(\widetilde{L}_n) = 2$ on $\mathbf{1}^\perp$ and exact random-permutation expectation $\mathbb{E}[\mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{line}}(S)] = \frac{2}{3}n^2(n^4-1)$. We also note (Remark 2.2) that enforcing linear dipole balance ($\langle X,Z\rangle = \langle Y,Z\rangle = 0$) and diagonal balance ($R_{D_\pm} = 0$) constrains only four directions in $\mathbf{1}^\perp$, leaving a $(2n-4)$-dimensional subspace of higher-degree polynomial row and column moments unconstrained, as illustrated at $n=4$ by an explicit permutation $S_{\mathrm{low}}$ with $\mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{line}}(S_{\mathrm{low}}) = 1{,}584$.

To resolve spatial structure inside the magic ground-state manifold $\ker(\widetilde{L}_n)$ where all 1D line sums vanish, we evaluate sliding $k\times k$ window entropies $\widetilde{H}_p(k)$ and record (Lemma 3.1) that for any even order $n=2m$, vanishing of the $(2m-1)^2$ interior $2\times 2$ quartet sums forces all $4m-1$ boundary-wrapping toroidal $2\times 2$ quartets to vanish, isolating the $48$ panmagic squares of Dudeney Group I at $n=4$. Across all $880$ order-$4$ magic squares, we prove the Concentric Shell Quadrupole Identity (Proposition 3.2), $M_{xyz}(S) = -8\,Q_{\mathrm{corner}}(S) - 3\,Q_{\mathrm{edge}}(S)$, which explains both the $48$ symmetry-protected octupole zeros on Group III and the $8$ accidental coprime-resonance zeros $(Q_{\mathrm{corner}}, Q_{\mathrm{edge}}) = (-3k, 8k)$ in Groups IX and X.

Turning to discrete and continuous relaxation dynamics, we apply the rank-$1$ transposition difference formula $\Delta\mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{line}}(\tau_{ab}) = -2d(g_a-g_b) + c_{ab}d^2$ ($c_{ab} \in \{2,\dots,7\}$, Lemma 4.1) to map all $9! = 362{,}880$ permutations of $S_9$ (Theorem 4.2). We prove that besides the $8$ Lo Shu ground states ($\mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{line}}=0$), $S_9$ possesses only $8$ spurious strict local minima forming a single $D_4$ orbit $S_{\mathrm{trap}}^{(3)}$ at $\mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{line}}=8$. On $S_{\mathrm{trap}}^{(3)}$, every single $2$-cycle ($\min\Delta\mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{line}} = +4$) and every single $3$-cycle ($\min\Delta\mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{line}} = +8$) strictly increases energy, but one and only one disjoint $(2,2)$ double swap—the row-preserving move $(6\leftrightarrow 7)(3\leftrightarrow 4)$, which crosses an intermediate single-swap saddle at $\mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{line}}^\ddagger = 12$ ($\Delta\mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{line}}^\ddagger = +4$)—escapes directly to $\mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{line}}=0$, expanding the deterministic ground-state basin of $S_9$ from $74.53\%$ to $100.00\%$. Finally, we demonstrate that naive 2D Gaussian-kernel particle relaxation fails ($0\%$ magic squares under both greedy cell snapping and exact $O(N^3)$ Hungarian bipartite projection due to convex amplitude averaging in the interior of the assignment polytope), whereas a discrete Multi-Exchange Iterated Local Search (ME-ILS) algorithm achieves $100.0\%$ ground-state convergence across all orders $n = 3, 4, \dots, 10$ (up to $100! \approx 9.33\times 10^{157}$ states), with mean restarts peaking at $n=5$ ($8.03$) and declining to $3.34$ at $n=10$ as the diagonal-free $(2,2)$ subspace fraction $f_{\mathrm{null}}(n) = 1 - \frac{2}{(n-1)^2}$ reaches $97.5\%$.
