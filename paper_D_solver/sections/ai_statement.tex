\section*{Acknowledgments and Declaration of Generative AI Use}
\label{sec:ai_statement}

This work grew out of the author's interactive physical and computational explorations of magic-square energy landscapes \cite{mathewson2026physicalsolver}, and forms the fourth part of the \emph{Magic Gems} series alongside \cite{mathewson2026gems,mathewson2026moments,mathewson2026mechanics}. Large language models (OpenAI's ChatGPT, Anthropic's Claude, and Google's Gemini) were used by the author as interactive assistants for symbolic checks, verification scripting, interactive simulator prototyping, and manuscript copy-editing following George M.~Whitesides's iterative outline-and-figure methodology \cite{whitesides2004writing}; all mathematical formulations, proofs, and exact computational certificates were verified by the author, who takes full responsibility for the work.
