\begin{abstract}
Every magic square of order $n$ is a zero-energy ground state of the line-indicator Hamiltonian $\Hline(S) = \sum_{L\in\mathcal{L}_n} R_L(S)^2 = \mathbf{z}^\top L_n \mathbf{z}$ on the symmetric group $S_{n^2}$, where $\mathbf{z} \in \R^{n^2}$ is the centred height field $Z_{ij} = 2s_{ij} - (n^2+1)$ and $L_n = A_n^\top A_n$ is the $(2n+2)$-line incidence Laplacian. While previous papers in this series established the convex-polytopal geometry of Magic Gems (Paper~I), their universal bivariate polynomial moments and isotropic inertia tensors (Paper~II), and their rigid-body Euler--Poinsot rotational mechanics (Paper~III), a fundamental inverse question remained open: how does the combinatorial energy landscape $\Hline : S_{n^2} \to \Z_{\ge 0}$ organize the $n^2!$ permutations around the magic ground-state manifold, and how can physical relaxation dynamics navigate its frustrated local minima? Here we resolve this question across four scales. First, we prove the \emph{Spectral Rationalization Theorem}: although $L_n$ on $\R^{n^2}$ possesses irrational eigenvalues $\lambda_\pm(n)$ arising from row--column--diagonal overlap, restricting $L_n$ to the $(n^2-1)$-dimensional zero-mean hyperplane $\mathbf{1}^\perp$ inhabited by all centred permutations decouples the constant mode and collapses the entire spectrum into exact integers---$\{0^{[n^2-2n-1]}, (n-2)^{[1]}, n^{[2n-1]}\}$ for even $n \ge 4$ and $\{0^{[n^2-2n-1]}, (n-1)^{[2]}, n^{[2n-2]}\}$ for odd $n \ge 3$---with a universal mean eigenvalue $\bar{\mu}(\Ltilde_n) = 2$ and exact random-permutation ensemble expectation $\mathbb{E}[\Hline(S)] = 2\|\mathbf{z}\|^2 = \frac{2}{3}n^2(n^4-1)$. We also prove a \emph{Low-Mode Insufficiency Proposition}, constructing an explicit $4\times 4$ counterexample $S_{\mathrm{low}}$ whose dipole moments $\langle X,Z\rangle = \langle Y,Z\rangle = 0$ and main diagonals $R_{D_\pm} = 0$ vanish identically, yet which remains trapped at $\Hline(S_{\mathrm{low}}) = 1{,}584 > 0$ inside the $(2n-3)$-dimensional high-frequency residual subspace. Second, we introduce multiscale sliding-window energies and Shannon entropies $H_p(k)$ across all $880$ Fr\'enicle order-$4$ magic squares and constructive families at $n \in \{3,4,5,6,8\}$, proving that vanishing interior $2\times 2$ quartet energy ($W_{2\times 2}^{\mathrm{int}} = 0$, $9$ windows) is equivalent on magic squares to vanishing toroidal quartet energy ($W_{2\times 2}^{\mathrm{tor}} = 0$, $16$ windows) and holds on exactly the $48$ panmagic squares of Dudeney Group~I, while the mixed octupole moment $M_{xyz}$ vanishes on all $48$ Group~III associative squares plus exactly $8$ anomalous non-associative squares ($4$ each in Groups~IX and~X). Third, we derive the exact quadratic transposition law $\Delta\Hline(\tau_{ab}) = -2d(g_a - g_b) + c_{ab}d^2$ and perform an exhaustive census of all $9! = 362{,}880$ states of $S_9$, discovering that $n=3$ possesses \emph{exactly $8$ spurious strict local minima} forming a single dihedral orbit $S_{\mathrm{trap}}^{(3)}$ at $\Hline = 8$ that traps $25.47\%$ of $S_9$ under both $2$-cycle and $3$-cycle descent, yet escapes via a \emph{unique} row-preserving $(2,2)$ double swap $(6\leftrightarrow 7)(3\leftrightarrow 4)$ ($1$ of $378$ disjoint double swaps) that restores the deterministic ground-state basin of $S_9$ to $362{,}880 / 362{,}880$ ($100.00\%$). Fourth, we analyze continuous ``Bubble Trinity'' Langevin relaxation across four regimes, prove why greedy spatial cell-snapping fails to converge on its own, and introduce a \emph{Re-Anchored Hierarchical Multi-Cycle Settler} combining $O(1)$ incremental $2$-cycle steepest descent, whole-row/column permutations, pruned line-preserving $(2,2)$ double swaps, and top-$K$ general $(2,2)$ swaps to achieve \emph{$100.0\%$ ground-state synthesis across all orders $n = 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8$} (up to $64! \approx 1.27\times 10^{89}$ states).
\end{abstract}
