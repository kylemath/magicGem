\begin{abstract}
Every magic square of order $n$ is a zero-energy ground state of the line-indicator Hamiltonian $\Hline(S) = \sum_{L\in\mathcal{L}_n} R_L(S)^2 = \mathbf{z}^\top L_n \mathbf{z}$ on the symmetric group $S_{n^2}$, where $\mathbf{z} \in \R^{n^2}$ is the centred height field $Z_{ij} = 2s_{ij} - (n^2+1)$ and $L_n = A_n^\top A_n$ is the $(2n+2)$-line incidence operator. While previous papers in this series established the convex-polytopal geometry of Magic Gems (Paper~I), their bivariate polynomial moments and isotropic inertia tensors (Paper~II), and their rigid-body rotational and static mechanics (Paper~III), a central inverse question remained open: how does the combinatorial energy landscape $\Hline : S_{n^2} \to \Z_{\ge 0}$ organize the $n^2!$ permutations around the magic ground-state manifold, why do naive continuous kernel relaxations and $2$- or $3$-exchange local searches get trapped, and what multi-exchange neighborhoods overcome those traps? Here we address this question across four scales. First, using the Rook's-graph decomposition of $L_n$, we show (\Cref{prop:spectral_rationalization}) that restricting $L_n$ to the $(n^2-1)$-dimensional zero-mean hyperplane $\mathbf{1}^\perp$ decouples the constant mode and reduces the irrational diagonal-coupled eigenvalues $\lambda_\pm(n)$ to exact integers---$\{0^{[n^2-2n-1]}, (n-2)^{[1]}, n^{[2n-1]}\}$ for even $n \ge 4$ and $\{0^{[n^2-2n-1]}, (n-1)^{[2]}, n^{[2n-2]}\}$ for odd $n \ge 3$---with mean eigenvalue $\bar{\mu}(\Ltilde_n) = 2$ and random-permutation expectation $\mathbb{E}[\Hline(S)] = 2\|\mathbf{z}\|^2 = \frac{2}{3}n^2(n^4-1)$, while linear dipole and diagonal balance ($\langle X,Z\rangle = \langle Y,Z\rangle = R_{D_\pm} = 0$) leaves a $(2n-4)$-dimensional subspace of higher-degree polynomial moments unconstrained (\Cref{rem:low_mode_insufficiency}). Second, we evaluate multiscale sliding-window energies and Shannon entropies $\widetilde{H}_p(k)$ across all $880$ Fr\'enicle order-$4$ magic squares and constructive families at $n \in \{3,4,5,6,8\}$, showing that vanishing interior $2\times 2$ quartet energy ($W_{2\times 2}^{\mathrm{int}} = 0$) is equivalent on even-order magic squares to vanishing toroidal quartet energy ($W_{2\times 2}^{\mathrm{tor}} = 0$, \Cref{lem:quartet_equivalence}), and proving the Concentric Shell Quadrupole Identity $M_{xyz}(S) = -8\,Q_{\mathrm{corner}}(S) - 3\,Q_{\mathrm{edge}}(S)$ (\Cref{prop:octupole_census}), which classifies all $56$ octupole zeros at order $4$ ($48$ associative squares in Group~III plus $8$ coprime-resonance squares in Groups~IX and~X). Third, we perform an exhaustive census of all $9! = 362{,}880$ permutations of $S_9$ (\Cref{thm:s9_traps}), proving that $n=3$ possesses \emph{exactly $8$ spurious strict $2$-exchange local minima} forming a single dihedral orbit $S_{\mathrm{trap}}^{(3)}$ at $\Hline = 8$ that traps $25.47\%$ of $S_9$ under both $2$-cycles and $3$-cycles, yet escapes across an intermediate single-swap saddle ($\Hline^\ddagger = 12, \Delta\Hline^\ddagger = +4$) via a \emph{unique} row-preserving $(2,2)$ double swap $(6\leftrightarrow 7)(3\leftrightarrow 4)$ ($1$ of $378$ disjoint double swaps) that restores the deterministic ground-state basin of $S_9$ to $100.00\%$. Fourth, we show that continuous 2D Gaussian-kernel drift fails ($0.0\%$) under both greedy cell snapping and exact $O(N^3)$ Hungarian bipartite projection due to convex amplitude averaging, whereas a discrete \emph{Multi-Exchange Iterated Local Search (ME-ILS)} combining $2$-exchange descent, line-preserving $(2,2)$ plaquette moves, candidate $(2,2)$ and row/column swaps, and thresholded basin-hopping achieves $100.0\%$ ground-state convergence across all orders $n = 3, 4, \dots, 10$ (up to $100! \approx 9.33\times 10^{157}$ states), with restart counts declining above $n=5$ as the diagonal-free $(2,2)$ subspace fraction $f_{\mathrm{null}}(n) = 1 - 2/(n-1)^2$ approaches unity.
\end{abstract}
