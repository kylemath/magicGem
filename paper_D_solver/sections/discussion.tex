\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

Across the four papers of the \emph{Magic Gems} series \cite{mathewson2026gems,mathewson2026moments,mathewson2026mechanics}, a unified picture emerges connecting discrete combinatorics, convex geometry, rigid-body mechanics, and statistical-mechanical energy landscapes. Whereas Papers~I--III showed how the magic line-sum constraints $\Hline(S) = 0$ sculpt the convex hull $K(S)$ (Paper~I), diagonalize the inertia tensor via bivariate moment orthogonality (Paper~II), and stabilize rigid-body rotation and tabletop resting equilibria (Paper~III), the present study demonstrates that the operator $L_n = A_n^\top A_n$ governing $\Hline(S) = \mathbf{z}^\top L_n \mathbf{z}$ possesses an equally rich spectral and topological structure away from the ground state. In particular, the collapse of the irrational eigenvalues $\lambda_\pm(n)$ of $L_n$ into exact integers $\{0, n-2, n\}$ (even $n$) or $\{0, n-1, n\}$ (odd $n$) on $\mathbf{1}^\perp$ (\Cref{thm:spectral_rationalization}) and the universal mean eigenvalue $\bar{\mu}(\Ltilde_n) = 2$ (\Cref{thm:ensemble_mean}) prove that the magic-square constraint operator is intrinsically rational on the hyperplane of centred permutations. At the same time, our Low-Mode Insufficiency Proposition (\Cref{prop:low_mode_insufficiency}) establishes that physical moment balance ($\langle X,Z\rangle = \langle Y,Z\rangle = 0$) and diagonal balance ($R_{D_\pm}=0$) constrain only four acoustic directions and leave a $(2n-4)$-dimensional zero-mean optical-phonon subspace ($2n-3$ in $\operatorname{im}(L_n)$) unconstrained.

Within the magic ground-state manifold $\ker(\Ltilde_n)$ itself, our census of sliding-window Shannon entropies $\widetilde{H}_p(k)$ and $2\times 2$ quartet energies (\Cref{thm:quartet_equivalence} and \Cref{prop:octupole_census}) reveals a second-tier spatial energy hierarchy. Vanishing interior $2\times 2$ quartet energy is equivalent on all even-order magic squares to vanishing toroidal quartet energy and isolates the $48$ Dudeney Group~I panmagic squares at $n=4$, while the Concentric Shell Quadrupole Identity $M_{xyz}(S) = -8\,Q_{\mathrm{corner}}(S) - 3\,Q_{\mathrm{edge}}(S)$ explains both the $48$ symmetry-protected octupole zeros of Group~III ($B_{\mathrm{comp}} = 0$) and the $8$ coprime-resonance octupole zeros in Groups~IX and~X ($B_{\mathrm{comp}} = 8$).

Finally, our exhaustive enumeration of $S_9$ (\Cref{thm:s9_traps}) and multi-order physical ablation (\Cref{tab:physical_analogs_ablation}) clarify why standard single-particle, $2$-body, and $3$-body physical relaxation dynamics fail on magic squares, and why cooperative $4$-body and planar modes succeed. Macroscopic torque balance is blind to the $2n-4$ zero-mean optical phonon modes (\Cref{prop:low_mode_insufficiency}); continuous colloidal drift fails ($0.0\%$) under both greedy snapping and exact Hungarian linear assignment because $\Hind(t)$ relaxes via convex amplitude averaging inside the Birkhoff polytope; Kawasaki $2$-spin exchange freezes behind the quadratic self-energy barrier $c_{ab}d^2$ ($20.0\%$ at $n=4$, $1.0\%$ at $n=5$); and Zener $3$-ring vacancy diffusion breaks row conservation ($0/8$ on $S_{\mathrm{trap}}^{(3)}$). By contrast, our \emph{Re-Anchored Hierarchical Multi-Cycle Settler} achieves $100.0\%$ ground-state synthesis across all orders $n = 3\dots 8$ (\Cref{tab:multi_order_settler}) by uniting Pauling spin-ice divergence-free $(2,2)$ loop moves ($\Delta\Hline^\ddagger = +4$ saddle on $S_{\mathrm{trap}}^{(3)}$) \cite{pauling1935structure,bramwell2001spin}, combinatorial $4$-body quadrupolar swaps \cite{weaire1984soap,falk1998dynamics}, crystallographic slip-plane glide ($2\binom{n}{2}$ row/column swaps), and Wales--Doye / GroEL chaperonin re-anchored basin-hopping \cite{wales1997global,thirumalai2001chaperonin}, governed by the Maxwell--Calladine ratio $\eta_{\mathrm{MC}}(n) = (n^2-2n-1)/(2n)$ and the $1 - 2/(n-1)^2$ diagonal-free $(2,2)$ subspace fraction \cite{maxwell1864calculation,calladine1978buckminster}.

Several natural open problems arise from these findings. First, what is the exact number of spurious $2$-cycle, $(2+3)$-cycle, and line-preserving $(2,2)$-cycle local minima across the full $16!$-state space of $S_{16}$, and what minimal cycle structure guarantees a $100\%$ zero-temperature basin on $S_{16}$ as $(2,2)$ double swaps do on $S_9$ (\Cref{thm:s9_traps})? Second, can the Spectral Rationalization Theorem (\Cref{thm:spectral_rationalization}) be extended to three-dimensional magic cubes $S_{n^3}$ \cite{rogers2004inertia,rogers2005multipole} and toroidal panmagic Laplacians? Third, can a continuous Birkhoff-polytope relaxation of $\mathbf{z}^\top L_n \mathbf{z}$ over doubly stochastic permutation matrices replace greedy 2D spatial snapping so that continuous trajectories project barrier-free onto $\ker(\Ltilde_n)$ at high orders $n \ge 6$? All interactive simulators, verification scripts, and datasets supporting this work are openly available for further exploration.
