\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

Across the four papers of the \emph{Magic Gems} series \cite{mathewson2026gems,mathewson2026moments,mathewson2026mechanics}, a coherent picture emerges connecting discrete combinatorics, convex geometry, rigid-body mechanics, and permutation energy landscapes. Whereas Papers~I--III showed how the magic line-sum constraints $\Hline(S) = 0$ sculpt the convex hull $K(S)$ (Paper~I), diagonalize the inertia tensor via bivariate moment orthogonality (Paper~II), and stabilize rigid-body rotation and tabletop resting equilibria (Paper~III), the present study examines the operator $L_n = A_n^\top A_n$ and the quadratic landscape $\Hline(S) = \mathbf{z}^\top L_n \mathbf{z}$ away from the ground state. Restricting the Rook's-graph-plus-diagonals decomposition of $L_n$ \cite{godsil2001algebraic,brouwer2012spectra,loly2009magic} to the zero-mean hyperplane $\mathbf{1}^\perp$ (\Cref{prop:spectral_rationalization}) yields exact integer eigenvalues $\{0, n-2, n\}$ (even $n$) or $\{0, n-1, n\}$ (odd $n$) with mean eigenvalue $\bar{\mu}(\Ltilde_n) = 2$ and random-permutation expectation $\mathbb{E}[\Hline(S)] = 2\|\mathbf{z}\|^2 = \frac{2}{3}n^2(n^4-1)$, while linear dipole and diagonal balance ($\langle X,Z\rangle = \langle Y,Z\rangle = R_{D_\pm} = 0$) leaves $2n-4$ higher-degree polynomial row and column moments unconstrained (\Cref{rem:low_mode_insufficiency}).

Within the magic ground-state manifold $\ker(\Ltilde_n)$ itself, our census of sliding-window Shannon entropies $\widetilde{H}_p(k)$ and $2\times 2$ quartet energies (\Cref{lem:quartet_equivalence} and \Cref{prop:octupole_census}) resolves a second-tier spatial hierarchy. Vanishing interior $2\times 2$ quartet energy is equivalent on all even-order magic squares to vanishing toroidal quartet energy and isolates the $48$ Dudeney Group~I panmagic squares at $n=4$, while the Concentric Shell Quadrupole Identity $M_{xyz}(S) = -8\,Q_{\mathrm{corner}}(S) - 3\,Q_{\mathrm{edge}}(S)$ explains both the $48$ symmetry-protected octupole zeros of Group~III ($B_{\mathrm{comp}} = 0$) and the $8$ coprime-resonance octupole zeros in Groups~IX and~X ($B_{\mathrm{comp}} = 8$).

Finally, our exhaustive enumeration of $S_9$ (\Cref{thm:s9_traps}) and multi-order ablation (\Cref{tab:physical_analogs_ablation}) clarify the roles of continuous versus discrete move neighborhoods on $\Hline(S)$. Naive 2D Gaussian-kernel drift fails ($0.0\%$) under both greedy cell snapping and exact $O(N^3)$ Hungarian bipartite projection because $\Hind(t)$ relaxes via convex amplitude averaging in the interior of the assignment polytope; $2$-exchange steepest descent traps behind the quadratic self-energy barrier $c_{ab}d^2$ ($20.0\%$ at $n=4$, $1.0\%$ at $n=5$); and $3$-exchange moves break row/column conservation ($0/8$ on $S_{\mathrm{trap}}^{(3)}$). By contrast, our discrete \emph{Multi-Exchange Iterated Local Search (ME-ILS)} achieves $100.0\%$ ground-state synthesis across all orders $n = 3\dots 10$ (\Cref{tab:multi_order_settler}) by combining $2$-exchange descent, row/column-preserving $(2,2)$ plaquette moves (which provide the unique escaping $(2,2)$ move across the $\Delta\Hline^\ddagger = +4$ single-swap saddle on $S_{\mathrm{trap}}^{(3)}$, analogous to divergence-free $4$-body loop moves in ice-type models \cite{pauling1935structure,bramwell2001spin}), candidate-list $(2,2)$ and row/column swaps, and threshold-bounded basin-hopping \cite{wales1997global}. Across orders $n=3\dots 10$, mean restarts peak at $n=5$ ($8.03$) and decline to $3.34$ at $n=10$ as the diagonal-free $(2,2)$ subspace fraction $f_{\mathrm{null}}(n) = 1 - 2/(n-1)^2$ reaches $97.5\%$.

Several natural open problems arise from these findings. First, what is the exact number of spurious $2$-exchange and line-preserving $(2,2)$-exchange local minima across the full $16!$-state space of $S_{16}$, and what minimal cycle neighborhood guarantees a $100\%$ single-quench basin on $S_{16}$ as $(2,2)$ double swaps do on $S_9$ (\Cref{thm:s9_traps})? Second, how does the zero-mean spectrum of \Cref{prop:spectral_rationalization} generalize to three-dimensional magic cubes $S_{n^3}$ \cite{rogers2004inertia,rogers2005multipole} and pandiagonal Laplacians? Third, in place of naive 2D spatial kernel interpolation, can a principled concave quadratic-assignment relaxation $\min_{P \in \mathcal{B}_{n^2}} \tr(P^\top L_n P \mathbf{z}\mathbf{z}^\top) - \gamma \|P\|_F^2$ over the Birkhoff polytope $\mathcal{B}_{n^2}$ of doubly stochastic matrices converge directly to permutation vertices in $\ker(\Ltilde_n)$ via Sinkhorn entropic annealing? All interactive simulators, verification scripts, and datasets supporting this work are openly available for further exploration.
