\begin{abstract}
Lifting an $n\times n$ arrangement $S$ of $\{1,\dots,n^2\}$ over the centred grid $A_n\subset\R^2$ produces $n^2$ three-dimensional vectors $\mathbf p_{ij}(\lambda)=(x_j,y_i,\lambda z_{ij})$ radiating from the origin $O$ and their convex hull, the \emph{Magic Gem} $\mathcal G_\lambda(S)$. We investigate when the combinatorial balance of a magic square translates into physical equilibrium, smooth rotation, and structural stability across four sculptural realizations: radial spoke stars and balance wheels from $O$, pivot-supported spinning tops, solid/skeletal/papercraft polyhedra, and freestanding stacked towers. First, by \emph{length--weight duality}, scaling vector lengths along unit rays $\hat{\mathbf p}_{ij}(\lambda)$ by $L_{ij}=R_{ij}(\lambda)^\beta$ is mechanically equivalent to weighting $\mathbf p_{ij}(\lambda)$ by $w_{ij}=R_{ij}(\lambda)^{\beta-1}$. For cubic 3D lengths ($\beta=3$), the vertical resultant $(\mathbf V_3)_z$ vanishes identically for every semi-magic square and every $\lambda>0$, while on the unit sphere $S^2$ ($\beta=0$), all $880$ order-four magic squares admit positive minimum-variance balancing weights $\mathbf w^*(S)>\mathbf 0$. In the plane, decomposing $A_4$ into three rings of radii $(\sqrt{2},\sqrt{10},3\sqrt{2})/2$ proves that planar unit vectors ($\alpha=0$) never balance on $\mathcal M_4$ because $r_2/r_1=\sqrt{5}\notin\Q$; the global minimum $18\sqrt{10}/5-8\sqrt{2}\approx0.07049$ is governed by the Pell convergent $9/4$ on eight Group~VI squares, while $204$ squares with parallel ring dipoles ($\kappa>0$) admit a second balance exponent $\alpha^*(\kappa)\ne1$ ($25$ at $\alpha^*=-1$ and $25$ at $\alpha^*=+3$). Second, equal point masses make every semi-magic square an axisymmetric rotor with spherical-top scale $\lambda_{\mathrm{iso}}=(n^2+1)^{-1/2}$ that spins torque-free on a central pivot under gravity, whereas entry masses $m_{ij}=s_{ij}$ split the horizontal moments by $2|Q_{xy}(S)|$ and trigger Dzhanibekov flips on $824$ squares. Third, we classify the $51$ naturally balanced solids ($\mathbf c_0=\mathbf 0$) and restore solid and skeletal balance via ``diamond-in-a-diamond'' convex layers $\mathcal G_0(S)\supset\mathcal G_1(S)$ ($35$ two-density solids, led by $8$ printable with modest excess density $\Delta\rho_1/\rho_0\in[5/4,40/19]$) and nested ball-and-rod wireframes $G(\mathcal G_0)\cup G(\mathcal G_1)$ ($158$ gems at $\lambda=1$). Moreover, we prove that the complete $\binom{N}{2}$-rod network $K_N$ satisfies $\mathbf c(K_N)=\mathbf 0$ and $\Sigma_{\mathrm{rods}}=\frac{2N-3}{3(N-1)}\Sigma(\lambda)$ ($\frac{29}{45}\Sigma(\lambda)$ at $n=4$), and show that every spanning-tree papercraft cutout net of $\partial\mathcal G_\lambda(S)$ has $N_{\mathrm{tabs}}=f_0-1$ glue tabs and unfolds without overlap across all $880$ gems. Finally, central inversion $\mathcal G_\lambda(r^2\overline S)=-\mathcal G_\lambda(S)$ locks any gem with centrally symmetric upper and lower facets onto its mirror twin to form zero-rotation vertical towers, led by $17$ knight's-move gems (such as \#763).
\end{abstract}
