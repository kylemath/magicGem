Highlights

- Axial moment conditions for magic squares link to Prouhet-Tarry-Escott systems.
- The permutation moment threshold satisfies sqrt(2n) <= k*(n) <= 2 sqrt(2n log n).
- Exact threshold values k*(n) are determined for all orders 3 <= n <= 40.
- Exact variances and limit laws are derived for random permutation line energies.
- Exactly 14,639,440 order-4 arrangements satisfy the four-term energy E_4 = 0.
