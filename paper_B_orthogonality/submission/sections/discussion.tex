\section{Open questions}
\label{sec:discussion}

The main results are the two-sided bound $(\sqrt2-o(1))\sqrt n\le k^*(n)\le(2\sqrt{\pi e}+o(1))\sqrt{n\log n}$ for the permutation threshold, with exact values for $n\le40$, and exact second moments of the line-sum energies of a random arrangement.
The following questions remain.

\begin{enumerate}\setlength\itemsep{2pt}
\item \emph{The order of $k^*(n)$.} Is $k^*(n)\asymp\sqrt n$?
The upper bound uses only that $\gamma$ is a nonzero integer vector with $\|\gamma\|_1\le n^4/4$, and for such vectors no bound of smaller order than $\sqrt n$ is possible (\Cref{sec:kstar_upper}); the factor $\sqrt{\log n}$ corresponds to the gap left open in \cite{BEK1999} for polynomials with coefficients in $\{-1,0,1\}$.
For $14\le n\le40$ the column-feasibility inequalities \eqref{eq:col_feasible} were the only obstruction (\Cref{sec:kstar_exact}).  Is this true for all $n$, and is $k^*(n)$ nondecreasing?
\item \emph{The size of the low-order class.} Is $|L_n|\sim N!\,(2\pi)^{-2}\det(\Sigma_U)^{-1/2}\,c_n$ with $c_n$ bounded away from $0$ and $\infty$, i.e.\ does a local limit theorem hold for the four statistics $(U_1,\dots,U_4)$ of \Cref{sec:lowmode_n4}?  The ratio $c_n$ is $0.59$, $0.80$ for $n=3,4$ and about $0.76$, $0.78$ for $n=5,6$.
\item \emph{Unit-vector balance.} Is there a magic square of some order whose ring imbalance $|\sum_cz_cu_c|$ (\Cref{sec:balance}) vanishes?  None exists for $n=3$ or $n=4$.
\item \emph{Other families.} The orthogonality formulation applies to pandiagonal and Franklin squares \cite{loly2007franklin}, with $W$ replaced by the span of the relevant line indicators.  The corresponding thresholds and random energies have not been worked out.
\end{enumerate}
