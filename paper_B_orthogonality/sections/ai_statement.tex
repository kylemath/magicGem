\section*{Use of AI systems}
\label{sec:ai_statement}

This paper was developed with extensive use of generative AI, and we state how so that readers can weigh the results accordingly.
The author posed the questions, starting from the orthogonality formulation of the line conditions in the companion paper \cite{mathewson_gems}, chose the directions to pursue, and is responsible for the content.
Large language models (Anthropic's Claude, used through the agentic tool Claude Code in October 2026) did most of the technical work under the author's direction: they proposed and wrote out proofs and constructions, wrote and ran all of the code in \texttt{anc/}, searched the literature, and drafted and revised the text.
Separate AI sessions then acted as adversarial referees; their objections and the changes made in response are recorded, and the record is available from the author.
AI systems are not authors of this paper.

Because of this, no result relies on an AI system being correct.
Every number in the paper is recomputed by the scripts in \texttt{anc/}, in exact arithmetic where stated.
The computer-assisted results, namely the upper bounds on $k^*(n)$ for $18\le n\le40$ and the count $|L_4|$, were each obtained by two separately written implementations; both implementations of each were themselves written with AI assistance, so they are independent of each other but not of the tools.
The upper bounds on $k^*(n)$ are backed by a certificate (\texttt{anc/kstar\_certificates.json}) that a standard-library script of under 40 lines checks in exact arithmetic, so that checking them does not require trusting the enumeration code.
