\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Reading the entries of a magic square as a height function on the centred grid makes the magic conditions the orthogonality of that function to a $2n$-dimensional space of test functions.
We have treated this as a viewpoint, not a result, and used it to obtain a moment hierarchy whose degree-two level implies Loly's inertia invariance and whose permutation threshold drops below the Vandermonde value $n-1$ from $n=14$ on; a balance theorem for the signed mass, with a sharp quantitative form and a negative result for unit-vector balance; exact moments and a limit law for the energies of a random arrangement, and the null distribution of Loly's moment of inertia; an exact single-swap formula with gap spectrum $d^2m/n^4$; and the exact count $14{,}639{,}440$ of $4\times4$ arrangements satisfying the four low-mode conditions.
What remains is a small set of exact statements about a classical object, together with the polyhedral companion paper~A \cite{mathewson_gems} and the open questions of \Cref{sec:discussion}.
