Suggested Reviewers
===================

Since this paper bridges Combinatorics, Polyhedral Geometry, and Statistics, here are suggested reviewers with expertise in these specific intersections.

1. Matthias Beck
   Affiliation: San Francisco State University / Freie Universität Berlin
   Expertise: Polyhedral geometry, Ehrhart theory, magic squares.
   Relevance: Author of "Computing the Continuous Discretely" and papers on magic square polyhedra.

2. Peter D. Loly
   Affiliation: University of Manitoba (Emeritus)
   Expertise: Scientific properties of magic squares, eigenvalues, moment of inertia.
   Relevance: Highly cited in your work; focuses on physical/scientific properties of magic squares.

3. Jesús De Loera
   Affiliation: University of California, Davis
   Expertise: Convex geometry, optimization, combinatorics.
   Relevance: Expert in the geometry of lattice points and polyhedra, which matches your "Magic Gem" construction.

4. Walter Trump
   Affiliation: (Independent Researcher, Germany)
   Expertise: Enumeration of magic squares.
   Relevance: World expert on counting magic squares; provided the enumeration data used in the field.

5. Heng Huat Chan
   Affiliation: National University of Singapore
   Expertise: Number theory, magic squares eigenvalues.
   Relevance: Cited in your work regarding algebraic properties.

6. Alexander Barvinok
   Affiliation: University of Michigan
   Expertise: Convexity, combinatorics.
   Relevance: His book "A Course in Convexity" is a standard reference; expert in the geometry of combinatorial objects.
