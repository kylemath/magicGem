\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

The Magic Gem is the convex hull of a magic square's height function over the centred grid, the same polyhedron for all eight $D_4$-images of the square.
Reading the entries as a height function makes the magic conditions the orthogonality of that function to a $2n$-dimensional space of test functions; we have treated this as a viewpoint, not a result, and used it to obtain a moment hierarchy whose degree-two level implies Loly's inertia invariance and whose permutation threshold drops below the Vandermonde value $n-1$ from $n=14$ on; exact moments and a limit law for the energies of a random arrangement, and the null distribution of Loly's moment of inertia; an exact single-swap formula with gap spectrum $d^2m/n^4$; the exact count $14{,}639{,}440$ of $4\times4$ arrangements satisfying the four low-mode conditions; and an exact computation of the geometry of the $880$ order-four gems, with a proof that Siamese gems have twelve vertices for every odd order.
Several statements of the previous version were artifacts of an asymmetric energy and are withdrawn (\Cref{sec:introduction}).
What remains is a small set of exact statements about a classical object, and a polyhedron that is pleasant to look at.
