## Reviews

### Review by Dr. Chelsea Chen

The manuscript presents an innovative approach to analyzing magic squares through geometric and topological methods. The authors' framework for embedding magic squares in R³ and subsequent analysis using persistent homology is both creative and mathematically sound.

#### Strengths:
1. The physical motivation for the R³ embedding is well-justified and provides intuitive understanding
2. The statistical analysis framework is robust and properly accounts for multiple testing
3. The connection between geometric properties and combinatorial constraints is well-established

#### Areas for Improvement:
1. The discussion of stratified structure could be strengthened by connecting to recent work in stratification learning
2. The persistence analysis could benefit from stability theorems to support the robustness of the findings

#### Recommendation: Accept with Minor Revisions
The paper makes a significant contribution to both discrete mathematics and applied topology. The revisions made in response to previous reviewers have substantially improved the manuscript.

### Review by Dr. Alexander Strang

This paper presents a novel perspective on magic squares through the lens of geometric and topological analysis. The methodology is sound and the results are interesting, though some theoretical aspects could be strengthened.

#### Strengths:
1. The computational framework is well-developed and reproducible
2. The dimension analysis provides new insights into the structure of magic squares
3. The physical interpretation adds valuable intuition to the mathematical framework

#### Areas for Improvement:
1. The theoretical foundations could be strengthened by connecting to recent work in geometric measure theory
2. The algorithmic complexity of the proposed methods should be discussed
3. More explicit connections to existing literature on magic square enumeration would be valuable

#### Recommendation: Accept with Moderate Revisions
The paper presents novel ideas and methods, but requires some additional theoretical development to reach its full potential.

### Review by Dr. Sarah Patel

The manuscript provides an interesting bridge between discrete mathematics and geometric topology through the study of magic squares. The approach is novel and the results are significant.

#### Strengths:
1. The combination of geometric, topological, and combinatorial perspectives is innovative
2. The empirical results are well-supported by statistical analysis
3. The visualization methods effectively communicate complex geometric concepts

#### Areas for Improvement:
1. The connection to combinatorial theory could be strengthened further
2. Additional examples of how the geometric perspective informs combinatorial properties would be valuable
3. The discussion of future applications could be expanded

#### Recommendation: Accept with Minor Revisions
The paper makes a valuable contribution to both discrete mathematics and applied topology, with clear potential for impact in both fields.

## Summary Recommendation

Based on the three reviews, the consensus is to **Accept with Minor to Moderate Revisions**. The paper presents novel and valuable contributions to both discrete mathematics and applied topology. The main revisions suggested are:

1. Strengthen theoretical connections to recent work in stratification learning and geometric measure theory
2. Add discussion of algorithmic complexity
3. Expand connections to combinatorial theory
4. Include additional examples demonstrating the practical value of the geometric perspective

The reviewers agree that the paper, once revised to address these points, will make a significant contribution to the field and merit publication in a high-impact journal.
