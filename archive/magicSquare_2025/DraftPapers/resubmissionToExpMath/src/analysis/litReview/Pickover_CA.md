Dear Dr. Mathewson,

I read your analysis of magic squares with great enthusiasm! As someone who has extensively explored the cultural and mathematical aspects of magic squares in my book "The Zen of Magic Squares, Circles, and Stars" (Pickover, 2002), I find your geometric and topological approach fascinating and complementary to existing research.

Your work reminds me of the unexpected connections I've often found between different mathematical concepts. In my research on strange attractors and pattern formation (Pickover, 1995), I've similarly found that geometric visualization can reveal hidden structures in mathematical objects.
